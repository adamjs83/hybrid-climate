# Hybrid Climate

[![HACS custom repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/HACS-Custom-41BDF5.svg)](https://hacs.xyz)
[![Latest release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/adamjs83/hybrid-climate)](https://github.com/adamjs83/hybrid-climate/releases)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-Apache%202.0-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

Hybrid Climate runs a whole house's heating and cooling as one system. You give it your rooms, their temperature
sensors and the thermostats or heat pumps that serve each room; it decides what runs, when, and at what setpoint,
so radiant floors, heat pumps and a central AC stop fighting each other.

<!-- screenshot: docs/user/images/card-overview.png (the card's Overview view) -->

## What it does for you

- **One climate entity per zone** with its own heating and cooling targets, plus a **master** entity for the whole
  house with Home, Away, Sleep, Vacation, Boost and Off.
- **Staged heating and cooling.** Stage 1 runs first; stage 2 joins when the room is far from target or stage 1 has
  run too long. Stage 2 adds to stage 1 instead of replacing it.
- **Shared equipment rules.** A heat pump that cools the basement cannot also be asked to heat the main floor; a
  zone can piggyback on a boiler another zone already started.
- **Outdoor limits.** No heating above an outdoor temperature, no cooling below one, house-wide or per zone, with a
  backup outdoor sensor list.
- **Holds.** Set a temperature on a zone's card and it stays until you release it, through mode changes, occupancy
  changes and restarts. Only Vacation ends every hold.
- **Wall thermostats that follow hand changes.** Turn the dial on the wall and the zone follows.
- **Regulation for slow systems.** Optional PI control for radiant floors, and three ways to correct a cooling
  unit that reads its own temperature wrong.
- **Occupancy and time-of-use.** Occupied and unoccupied temperatures per zone, and relaxed targets during peak
  electricity periods.
- **A dashboard card** that builds itself from your configuration, and Repairs issues when something needs you.

## What you get

| Entity | One per | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Master climate | house | Household mode (Home, Away, Sleep, Vacation, Boost, Off) and a summary of every zone |
| Zone climate | zone | Current temperature, heating and cooling targets, the `hold` preset |
| Hold switch | zone | On while the zone holds its current targets; off resumes the saved temperatures |
| Saved temperatures | zone | Number entities for Home, Away, Sleep, Vacation, Occupied and Unoccupied targets |
| PI gains | zone with regulation | Number entities you tune live, no reload |
| Temperature sensor | zone | The control temperature after aggregation and smoothing |
| Diagnostic sensors | zone and house | Reason, stage, temperature spread, uncontrolled devices, outdoor source |
| Restore control button | zone | Sends the idle command to devices that no zone is using right now |
| Dashboard card | dashboard | Overview, zone details, setpoints, PI controller and device views |

Entity ids are generated by Home Assistant from the names, so yours will differ from the examples in these docs.
The full list, with attributes, is in [Entities](docs/user/entities.md).

## Requirements

- Home Assistant with [HACS](https://hacs.xyz). This release is tested against Home Assistant 2026.9.
- Each device you want controlled must be a `climate` entity that accepts a target temperature and the `heat`,
  `cool` or `off` mode (a wall thermostat, a heat pump integration, a smart radiator valve).
- Temperature sensors as `sensor` entities, or let a zone use its device's own reading.
- Turn off the schedules on the thermostats Hybrid Climate will control, and retire automations that set their
  temperatures. The integration sends every setpoint from now on.
- **Temperatures are in °F.** Every default, bound and example in these docs is in °F; the integration is
  designed and tested on a Fahrenheit install.

## Installation

### HACS (recommended)

1. In HACS, open the menu (⋮) and choose **Custom repositories**.
2. Add `https://github.com/adamjs83/hybrid-climate` with category **Integration**.
3. Search for **Hybrid Climate** in HACS and download it.
4. Restart Home Assistant.
5. Go to **Settings → Devices & Services → Add Integration**, search for **Hybrid Climate**, and give the master
   entity a name.

### Manual

1. Copy `custom_components/hybrid_climate/` into your Home Assistant `config/custom_components/` folder.
2. Restart Home Assistant.
3. Add the integration as in step 5 above.

## Quick start

The [quick start](docs/user/quick-start.md) walks you through your first zone in about ten minutes: an outdoor
sensor, a room sensor, one thermostat in stage 1, and what appears after you press Save. In short:

1. **Configure → Global settings**: pick your outdoor temperature entity.
2. **Configure → Zones → + Add New Zone**: zone ID, display name, the room's temperature sensor.
3. **Heating stages**: tick **Enable heating**, pick the thermostat for stage 1, keep the threshold at 1.0.
4. **Cooling stages**: the same if the room can cool, otherwise leave it off. Press **Save**.
5. Find the new zone climate entity, set a temperature, and watch the thermostat follow.

Already have a YAML configuration? It is imported once when you add the integration; after that, everything is
managed in the UI. See [YAML](docs/user/yaml.md).

## Key ideas

- **Zones and stages.** A zone is a room or an area with one temperature. Its heating and cooling each have
  stages; a stage is a list of devices and the distance from target that starts it.
  → [Staging](docs/user/concepts/staging.md)
- **Modes and saved temperatures.** The master mode picks which saved temperatures every zone uses. The saved
  temperatures are number entities you can change from any dashboard.
  → [Presets and modes](docs/user/configuration/presets-and-modes.md)
- **Holds.** A temperature set on a zone's card is a hold; it wins over modes and occupancy until you release it.
  → [Holds](docs/user/concepts/holds.md)
- **Wall thermostats.** A device marked "follows hand changes" pushes its dial changes back into the zone.
  → [Wall thermostats](docs/user/concepts/wall-thermostats.md)
- **Idle and restore.** What a device is sent when its zone is satisfied, and how the integration takes devices
  back after a restart. → [Idle and restore](docs/user/concepts/idle-and-restore.md)
- **Outdoor limits.** → [Outdoor](docs/user/concepts/outdoor.md)
- **Regulation.** → [Heating PI](docs/user/regulation/heating-pi.md), [Cooling](docs/user/regulation/cooling.md)

## Troubleshooting

- **Logs:** Settings → System → Logs, filter for `hybrid_climate`. For more detail add this to `configuration.yaml`
  and restart:

  ```yaml
  logger:
    logs:
      custom_components.hybrid_climate: debug
  ```

- **Repairs:** Settings → System → Repairs lists a zone whose sensors stopped reporting, a device that has been
  unavailable too long, a thermostat changed by hand, and a stage with no usable device. Each clears itself.
- **Diagnostics:** on the integration's page, the ⋮ menu → **Download diagnostics** gives a snapshot of sensors,
  lockouts and every device's requested and reported state. Attach it to a bug report.
- Problems by symptom: [Troubleshooting](docs/user/troubleshooting.md).
